## About this game

**Build a bobblehead.** Pick a face, hair, eyes, mouth, a hat and a tiny body,
or paint your own body texture over the template. Then press Play, and become
a Big Head.

**Play with it.** Click it and it boings. Drag it and it stretches. Fling it
hard enough and it sees stars. Right-click for fireworks: every friend who
plays Big Head adds a rocket to your barrage.

**Collect.** Fireworks sometimes drop gift boxes: new hats, hair and bodies
in four styles each. Hunt for the super-rare unicorn set. Unlock the Balloon
and Marionette rigs and play with your head in whole new ways (pop goes the
head).

**Friends on the shelf.** Friends who play Big Head stand on your shelves as
little bobbleheads. Boop them.

**Desktop buddy.** Shrink your bobblehead into a tiny see-through window that
sits on your desktop while you work, watches your cursor, and now and then
drops a present.

- Customizable bobblehead with 100+ collectable items
- Springy physics: boop, drag, fling, pop
- Fireworks that grow with your friends list
- Balloon and Marionette rigs to unlock
- Desktop buddy mode
- Steam achievements
